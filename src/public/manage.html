Delete Integration?

This will soft delete the Jira integration:

  • All sync jobs will be paused immediately.
  • Your data will be retained for 30 days before permanent deletion.
  • You can restore this integration within the retention window.
⚠ After 30 days all tokens, credentials, and sync data will be permanently purged.
⚠ Permanently Delete Integration?

This action is irreversible.

A hard delete will immediately and permanently purge:

  • All OAuth tokens and credentials
  • All sync data and history
  • All lifecycle events and audit logs
⚠ This cannot be undone. Available in sandbox environments only.
